**Rule:** every row lands a **workflow half and a surface half together**. A
row is done only when both halves pass live on M4 (ACS) or M2 (CVP). A row
whose workflow works but whose surface is still OPEN is *half-Landed*, and it
stays the open row. The two trains run in parallel with one open Grok Land
each, and a train pulls its next row the moment a Land proves.

careful (one at a time, inputs only) · `COUNCIL` = mock or gate · `PROOF` =
