## What we deliberately do NOT take

- Consumer-viral framing ("find viral outliers", growth-hacker tone) — we're a professional production OS (teardown §2).
- Gradient cover cards as identity — color stays state-only here; taxonomy may exist without the rainbow.
- Channel/outlier monitoring — off-mission until E4 (after El Paso + edit/review depth).
